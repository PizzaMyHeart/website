[The Russo-Ukrainian War](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/63326676) by Serhii Plokhy


Fascinating, readable take on the historical and current driving forces behind Russia's invasion of Ukraine, and its rippling effects on the rest of the world. The second half does devolve a bit into a list of military engagements, and the portrayal of the Ukrainian army feels uncomfortably close to hagiography&mdash;granted, it is impossible to sympathise with Russia in this conflict. But I found this to be an enlightening and enjoyable read overall, including the closing chapters that step into the implications for the US-China rivalry.
